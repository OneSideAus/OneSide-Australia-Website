The OneSide Assessment

You're the expert on your club.
We're the experts on what's required.

Tell us what you do and how you do it. OneSide measures it against national and sport-specific requirements and produces a gap report with practical options for what child safety looks like in your club's day-to-day operations.

What's in your report

A complete picture of where your club stands.

Your gap report is generated immediately after you complete the assessment. Here's exactly what it contains.

Assessment against every child safety standard

Every standard in your state's child safe framework is assessed individually. Each one receives a clear finding: Gap, Partial, or Met — so you know exactly where you stand across every area, not just the obvious ones.

Covers your state's standards, the National Principles, the National Integrity Framework, and your sport-specific framework.

A comprehensive summary of where your club sits

The report opens with a plain-English summary of your club's overall child safety position — written specifically for your club, your sport, and your state. Not a template. Not a generic output.

Your report is yours to keep, share with your committee, and use as your annual benchmark.

Practical options to address every gap

For every gap or partial finding, the report gives you 3 to 4 practical options — scaled from a quick win to a more complete solution. Each option is written for how your club actually runs, not how a policy manual assumes it does.

Options, not mandates. Your committee decides what to take on and in what order.

A prioritised action list based on your biggest risks

The report concludes with a Priority Actions list — the 3 to 5 things your club should focus on first, ranked by risk and impact. So you're not staring at 11 standards wondering where to begin. You have a clear starting point.

Quick wins first, bigger structural changes later. Built around what your club can realistically do.
